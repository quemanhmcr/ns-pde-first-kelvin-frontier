# Intrinsic max-normalized enstrophy localization and the NS compatibility grammar

This milestone asks for an endogenous localization rule before asking for a first-bad
threshold.  The rule is not invented outside Navier--Stokes.  It is generated by the
literal enstrophy field itself:

\[
e(x,t)=\frac12|\omega(x,t)|^2,
\qquad
M(t)=\max_{x\in\mathbb T^3} e(x,t),
\qquad
g(x,t)=\frac{e(x,t)}{M(t)}.
\]

Whenever `M>0`, the entire nested family

\[
\Omega_\theta(t)=\{x:g(x,t)\ge\theta\},\qquad 0<\theta<1,
\]

is therefore supplied by the physical PDE state.  No absolute badness threshold,
packet coordinate, critical-branch label, or external selector force is needed to
define this filtration.

This is a **candidate localization grammar**, not a continuation criterion.  The
remaining theorem would have to show that a loss of classical continuation is forced
to manifest through this filtration together with the physical Kelvin/Nanson/support
state.  That identification remains Open-literal/Open.

## 1. Exact NS similarity grammar

For the standard Navier--Stokes similarity action

\[
u_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda u(\lambda x,\lambda^2 t),
\qquad
p_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda^2p(\lambda x,\lambda^2t),
\]

with the same viscosity, the relevant physical weights are

\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}
 &x&t&u&\omega&e&\nabla e&e_t&V_{\partial\Omega}\\ \hline
\text{weight}&-1&-2&1&2&4&5&6&1.
\end{array}
\]

The local enstrophy faces

\[
\omega\!\cdot S\omega,
\qquad
\nu|\nabla\omega|^2,
\qquad
\nu\Delta e
\]

all have weight `6`.  Hence

\[
M_\lambda(t)=\lambda^4M(\lambda^2t),
\qquad
g_\lambda(x,t)=g(\lambda x,\lambda^2t).
\]

For integer `lambda=n`, periodic data on the `2 pi` torus remain periodic on the same
torus.  Thus this is not merely dimensional rhetoric; exact periodic NS solutions
realize the scaling family.

**Classification: Exact identity / exact NS symmetry typing.**

## 2. Absolute first-bad magnitudes are not intrinsic

\[

\qquad
M_n=\frac{A^2n^4}{2}e^{-2\nu n^2t}.

At `t=0`, `M_n~n^4`.  On any regular normalized level

\]

Therefore no fixed finite threshold on raw enstrophy or raw Kelvin-bulk magnitude can

This strengthens the earlier amplitude calibrations: here the obstruction is the
actual NS similarity grammar on a fixed periodic domain.

**Classification: Audited exact-NS calibration / rigorous absolute-threshold no-go.**

## 3. The max envelope needs no branch selector

For smooth `e` on the compact torus, define the active maximum set

\[
\mathcal A(t)=\{x:e(x,t)=M(t)\}.
\]

Uniform first-order expansion in time on the compact spatial domain gives the
one-sided envelope laws

\[
D^+M(t)=\max_{x\in\mathcal A(t)}\partial_t e(x,t),
\]

\[
D^-M(t)=\min_{x\in\mathcal A(t)}\partial_t e(x,t).
\]

A short proof is intrinsic.  For the lower right bound, keep an active maximizer with
largest `e_t`; for the upper bound, take maximizers at `t+h`, pass to a convergent
subsequence, and use the uniform Taylor remainder.  The left derivative is the same
argument with `h<0`.

At every active spatial maximum,

\[
\nabla e=0,
\qquad
\Delta e\le0,
\]

and the literal NS enstrophy equation gives

\[
\partial_t e
=
\omega\!\cdot S\omega
-\nu|\nabla\omega|^2
+\nu\Delta e.
\]

Hence the one-sided dynamics of the global maximum are determined by the active-set
values of the same three physical faces.  A critical branch can switch, merge, or
accumulate without requiring a locally-finite branch-selector path for the scalar
`M(t)` itself.

This does **not** solve local finiteness for a full packet-valued hysteretic selector.
It removes that issue only from the scalar max envelope used to normalize the
localization filtration.

**Classification: Rigorous consequence of compact max-envelope calculus plus the exact NS enstrophy law.**

## 5. The boundary compatibility defect

Fix a regular boundary point of

\[
\partial\Omega_\theta(t): e=\theta M,
\qquad |\nabla e|>0.
\]

For the superlevel set, the outward unit normal is

\[
n_{\rm out}=-\frac{\nabla e}{|\nabla e|}.
\]

Let `V_n` be the outward normal velocity of the moving level.  Differentiating
`e-theta M=0` on the boundary gives

\[
V_n=\frac{e_t-\theta\dot M}{|\nabla e|}.
\]

Write the material NS enstrophy rate as

\[
R
=
\omega\!\cdot S\omega
-\nu|\nabla\omega|^2
+\nu\Delta e.
\]

Since

\[
e_t+u\cdot\nabla e=R,
\qquad
u\cdot\nabla e=-|\nabla e|\,u\cdot n_{\rm out},
\]

we obtain the exact physical law

\[
\boxed{
V_n-u\cdot n_{\rm out}
=
\frac{\mathcal C_\theta}{|\nabla e|}
}
\]

with

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_\theta
=
\omega\!\cdot S\omega
-\nu|\nabla\omega|^2
+\nu\Delta e
-\theta\dot M.
}
\]

This is the central compatibility object of the milestone.

It is not an extra force.  It is the difference between the **local material rate**
required by NS and the **global normalization rate** required to remain on the same
max-normalized level.

`C_theta=0` means that normalized level is material in its normal direction.
`C_theta!=0` means the Eulerian observation surface slips through the fluid.

**Classification: Exact identity / exact physical interpretation.**

## 6. Similarity-invariant grammar variables

\boxed{

Thus the scale-free boundary grammar is not a raw magnitude; it is a ratio of two
objects whose weights are forced by NS.

**Classification: Exact identity / exact similarity normalization.**

## 9. Exact four-mode global-max crossing

To audit the max envelope on its literal global domain, use the exact heat-shear

\[

+4e^{4-4\nu t}\cos2y

+2e^{16-16\nu t}\cos4y.

It is generated by an exact periodic x-shear velocity and solves `q_t=nu q_yy`.
At

\[
t_*=1/\nu,
\]

write `c=cos y`.  Then

\[
q_*(c)=16c^4-4c^3-8c^2+4c-2.
\]

The global upper certificate is exact:

\[
6-q_*(c)
=4(1-c^2)(4c^2-c+2)\ge0.

For the lower side,

\[
\frac{6+q_*(c)}4
=4(c^2-1/4)^2+3/4+c(1-c^2),

\[
|c(1-c^2)|\le\frac{2}{3\sqrt3}<\frac34.

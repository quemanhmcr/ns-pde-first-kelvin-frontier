This note asks one level deeper whether the many familiar spectral balances are
still being treated separately.  The answer is again no.

single exact scale flux.  Navier--Stokes obeys a one-dimensional continuity equation

- kinetic energy is the total mass of the density;

- the positive critical `H^(1/2)` quadratic is the negative-half moment of the same

Thus energy cascade, stretching, critical chirality transfer and viscous dissipation

No shell partition, selector, packet score, continuation hypothesis or phenomenology

## 1. The canonical heat-resolved energy

For heat age `h>=0` define

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E(h,t)
:=\frac12\langle u(t),P_hu(t)\rangle
=\frac12\|P_{h/2}u(t)\|_2^2.
}
\]

This is not a shell energy.  It is the unique heat-functional-calculus resolution of
the actual kinetic energy.

Because the mean velocity has been removed,

\[
\mathcal E(0,t)=\frac12\|u(t)\|_2^2,
\qquad
\mathcal E(h,t)\to0
\quad(h\to\infty).
\]

Differentiate in heat age:

\[
\partial_h\mathcal E
=-\frac12\langle u,AP_hu\rangle.
\]

Define the positive heat-scale density

\[
\boxed{
\rho(h,t)
:=-\partial_h\mathcal E(h,t)
=\frac12\|A^{1/2}P_{h/2}u(t)\|_2^2
\ge0.
}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\int_0^\infty\rho(h,t)\,dh
=\frac12\|u(t)\|_2^2.
}
\]

At the heat-age-zero boundary,

\[
\boxed{
\rho(0,t)
=\frac12\|A^{1/2}u\|_2^2
=\frac12\|\omega\|_2^2
=:Z(t).
}
\]

Thus kinetic energy is the total heat-scale mass and enstrophy is its boundary
density.

**Classification: Exact Hodge heat-functional-calculus identities.**

## 2. One canonical nonlinear scale flux

Define

\[
\boxed{
\Pi(h,t)

The sign convention is chosen so positive `Pi` contributes positively to forward

\[
\boxed{

At positive heat age, `Pi` need not vanish because heat smoothing does not preserve
the pointwise product.

**Classification: Exact definition from the literal Lamb current and exact boundary
nullity.**

## 3. Navier--Stokes is a continuity equation on the Hodge heat half-line

Differentiate `E(h,t)` in physical time.  Self-adjointness of `P_h` gives

\[
\partial_t\mathcal E
=\langle\partial_tu,P_hu\rangle.
\]

Insert Navier--Stokes:

\[
\partial_t\mathcal E
=\langle u\times\omega,P_hu\rangle
-\nu\langle Au,P_hu\rangle.
\]

Using the definitions of `Pi` and `partial_h E`,

\[
\boxed{
\partial_t\mathcal E
-2\nu\,\partial_h\mathcal E
=-\Pi.
}
\]

Differentiate this identity in `h` and use `rho=-partial_h E`.  One obtains

\[
\boxed{
\partial_t\rho
-\partial_h\bigl(2\nu\rho+\Pi\bigr)
=0.
}
\]

This is an exact one-dimensional continuity equation on

\[
h\in[0,\infty).
\]

With the conventional orientation `rho_t+partial_h J_h=0`, the heat-scale current is

\[
J_h=-(2\nu\rho+\Pi).
\]

The viscous part is therefore a constant drift toward decreasing heat age, i.e.
toward the fine-scale boundary `h=0`; the nonlinear part is the self-generated flux
`Pi`.

Integrating over the heat half-line gives

\[
\frac d{dt}\int_0^\infty\rho\,dh
=-\bigl(2\nu\rho(0,t)+\Pi(0,t)\bigr).
\]

Since `Pi(0)=0`,

\[
\boxed{
\frac d{dt}\frac12\|u\|_2^2
=-2\nu\rho(0,t)
=-\nu\|\omega\|_2^2.
}
\]

Thus viscosity is the **only energy exit through the heat-age-zero boundary**.
Nonlinearity can transport positive energy density toward or away from that boundary
but cannot cross it directly.

**Classification: Exact heat-scale continuity law and exact recovery of the kinetic
energy identity.**

## 4. The nonlinear scale flux is itself a heat-null product defect

\[
\langle u\times\omega,P_hu\rangle

\[

\[

:=P_r(u\times\omega)-P_ru\times P_r\omega,

\[
\boxed{

=-\left\langle
P_{h/2}u,
\mathfrak D_{h/2}^\times(u,\omega)

Using the exact heat product-defect/carré-du-champ formula from the preceding note,

\partial_kP_qu\times\partial_kP_q\omega

\[
\boxed{

\partial_kP_qu\times\partial_kP_q\omega

Therefore the canonical scale flux is not an added cascade model.  It is the same

For every fixed smooth physical time,

\boxed{

because the heat product defect vanishes at zero heat age.

**Classification: Exact heat-null/carré-du-champ representation; rigorous smooth

## 5. Vortex stretching is the boundary slope of the same scale flux

**Classification: Exact boundary-flux identity / rigorous compression of stretching.**

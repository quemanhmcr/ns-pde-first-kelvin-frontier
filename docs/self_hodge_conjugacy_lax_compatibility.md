# Self-Hodge conjugacy / Lax compatibility

This note continues the compression programme below the separate pressure,
stretching, strain, Kelvin, and diffusion descriptions.  It introduces no score,
selector, packet criterion, or continuation hypothesis.  The question is whether
the several time-dependent Hodge objects that appeared in the previous two
milestones are genuinely separate pieces of dynamics.

They are not.

On every smooth Navier--Stokes interval the complete natural de Rham--Hodge
calculus in material coordinates is obtained from the fixed Euclidean Hodge
calculus by one and the same pullback conjugacy.  Differentially, every natural
operator obeys one universal Lax/commutator law.  Pressure, vortex stretching,
strain feedback, the moving Laplacian, the Hodge projector, Biot--Savart
reconstruction, and the carré-du-champ therefore do not have separate geometric
motion laws below this level.

The remaining self-interaction is smaller: the conjugating diffeomorphism is
generated by the velocity reconstructed from the same vorticity form that is being
Hodge-heated.

No no-escape, continuation, restart, or global-regularity theorem is claimed.

## 1. The material Hodge calculus is one conjugacy orbit

Let

\[
T_t:=\Phi_t^*
\]

be pullback by the actual Lagrangian flow, and let

\[
G_t=T_t g_0.
\]

If `A_g` is any diffeomorphism-natural operator between tensor/form bundles, then
naturality means

\[
A_{\psi^*g}\,\psi^*=\psi^*A_g.
\]

Taking `psi=Phi_t` gives the exact intertwining identity

\[
\boxed{
A_{G_t}T_t=T_tA_{g_0}.
}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
A_{G_t}=T_tA_{g_0}T_t^{-1}.
}
\]

This applies, with the appropriate input/output representations, to the Hodge star,
codifferential, Hodge Laplacian, Hodge projectors, Green/resolvent operators on the
orthogonal harmonic complement, the co-closed Biot--Savart primitive, the
Hodge--strain transform, and every natural tensor contraction/product built from
them.  Exterior differentiation is the metric-independent special case.

**Classification: Exact naturality/conjugacy identity.**

## 2. One universal Lax law generates every Hodge-operator derivative

Let

\[
U=T_tu=\Phi_t^*u
\]

be the pulled-back velocity vector field.  The standard derivative of pullback gives

\[
\dot T_tT_t^{-1}=\mathcal L_U.
\]

Differentiate the conjugacy identity of Section 1.  For an endomorphism on one
representation,

\[
\boxed{
\partial_tA_G=[\mathcal L_U,A_G].
}
\]

For an operator between different tensor degrees/types the same formula means

\[
\boxed{
\partial_tA_G
=\mathcal L_U^{\rm out}A_G-A_G\mathcal L_U^{\rm in}.
}
\]

Thus there is one operator connection

\[
\boxed{
\nabla_t^{\rm op}A_G
:=\partial_tA_G-\operatorname{ad}_{\mathcal L_U}A_G=0
}
\]

for the whole natural Hodge calculus.

In particular,

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\partial_t *_G&=[\mathcal L_U,*_G],\\
\partial_t\delta_G&=[\mathcal L_U,\delta_G],\\
\partial_t\Delta_G&=[\mathcal L_U,\Delta_G],\\
\partial_tP_G&=[\mathcal L_U,P_G].
\end{aligned}
}
\]

Since `[L_U,d]=0`, the de Rham differential is parallel under the same connection.
The preceding pressure and stretching formulas are therefore not two fortunate
commutator identities.  They are two components of one universal naturality law.

**Classification: Exact Lax/commutator identity.**

## 3. Pressure is the projector component of the universal Lax law

The material velocity one-form obeys

\[
P_G\bar\alpha=\bar\alpha,
\qquad
\partial_t\bar\alpha-\nu\Delta_G\bar\alpha=d\bar B.
\]

The previous milestone showed

\[
d\bar B=\dot P_G\bar\alpha.
\]

The universal Lax law sharpens this to

\[
\boxed{
d\bar B=[\mathcal L_U,P_G]\bar\alpha.}
\]

Because `P_G bar alpha=bar alpha`,

\[
\boxed{
d\bar B=(I-P_G)\mathcal L_U\bar\alpha.}
\]

Thus the Bernoulli/pressure exact force is precisely the exact Hodge component of
the self-Lie transport of the velocity one-form.  It is gauge on a closed Kelvin
current and simultaneously the constraint force that keeps the velocity in the
co-closed Hodge range.

There is no contradiction between those two statements: they are different
representations of the same projector commutator.

**Classification: Exact consequence.**

**Classification: Rigorous structural consequence.**

## 13. Functorial exhaustion: no further natural Hodge mechanism can appear

The conjugacy theorem is also a stopping theorem for the architecture.

the material metric, the de Rham differential, Hodge star/codifferential,
Hodge-Laplacian functional calculus, canonical Green/Biot--Savart maps, tensor

`L_U` representation commutator.

If the construction is nonlinear in the evolving state, additional terms can arise
only from applying its ordinary product/chain rule to the state equation.  At second
order the genuinely non-derivation piece is exactly the already-identified
Bochner/carre-du-champ defect of `Delta_G`.  Closed-current boundary terms remain
typed by `d`/Stokes, and moving restrictions remain Reynolds/readout faces rather

Thus within this natural Hodge/current grammar there is no unexplored independent
``pressure mechanism'', ``stretching mechanism'', ``metric mechanism'', ``Kelvin
mechanism'', or ``contact mechanism'' waiting to be discovered by adding another
representation.  A future genuinely new primitive would have to enter outside this
functorial closure and be derived literally from Navier--Stokes, not declared by the
architecture.

This is precisely why continuing case-by-case inside the existing Hodge grammar is
now mathematically unjustified: the universal conjugacy theorem has already fixed
how every such natural geometric representation moves.

**Classification: Rigorous functorial-exhaustion consequence of the exact
conjugacy law.**

\text{Hodge/Biot--Savart primitive, an unbounded conjugating diffeomorphism}\

parabolic evolution suffices to exclude escape is **Conjectural/Open**.

No restart, continuation, or global regularity statement follows from this note.

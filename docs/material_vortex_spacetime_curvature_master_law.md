# Material--vortex spacetime curvature master law

## Purpose

This note resets the operator frontier to objects forced directly by the literal
incompressible Navier--Stokes equation.  It does not introduce a selector, spectral
population, Rayleigh score, heat-age action, or abstract Poisson model.

Write

\[
\alpha=u^\flat,
\qquad
\beta=d\alpha=\iota_\omega\operatorname{vol},
\qquad
\delta\alpha=0,
\]

and use the positive Hodge Laplacian

\[
\Delta_H=d\delta+\delta d=-\Delta_{\rm PDE}.
\]

The one-form momentum equation is

\[
(\partial_t+\mathcal L_u+\nu\Delta_H)\alpha=dB,
\qquad
B=\frac{|u|^2}{2}-p.
\]

Cartan's identity shows that the already-existing vorticity current

\[
\mathcal J_{\rm NS}:=\iota_u\beta+\nu\delta\beta
\]

is exactly the time-space curvature component of the same momentum one-form.  Set

\[
\varphi:=B-\alpha(u)
\]

and, on physical spacetime \(I\times\mathbb T^3\), define only

\[
\mathfrak A:=\alpha+\varphi\,dt.
\]

Then literal NS forces

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak F:=d_{t,x}\mathfrak A
=\beta-dt\wedge\mathcal J_{\rm NS}.
}
\]

Thus the spacetime two-form is not an added state.  It is simply the exterior
derivative of the momentum potential already supplied by NS, and its temporal
component is exactly the de Rham current already present in the vorticity equation.

The primitive law has two faces:

\[
\boxed{d_{t,x}\mathfrak F=0}
\]

and, for the actual material spacetime vector

\[
V:=\partial_t+u,
\]

\[
\boxed{
\left.\iota_V\mathfrak F\right|_{T\mathbb T^3}
=-\nu\delta\beta.
}
\]

The first is Bianchi/closedness.  The second says that in the frame generated by the
same velocity, the entire Euler current cancels; the only remaining time-space
curvature is the Hodge-adjoint viscous current.

This one law contains the earlier de Rham current equation, material Hodge heat,
viscous Kelvin circulation, the Euler vortex-worldsheet commuting law, and the local
helicity transgression.  None is promoted as a new primitive below.

It still does **not** prove no-escape.  Exact arbitrarily high-frequency Beltrami heat
modes make the raw kernel defect and spacetime Pfaffian arbitrarily large while
remaining globally smooth.  The remaining frontier is therefore not the magnitude
of the curvature defect but the cumulative self-generated transverse coherence of
the same material/vortex/current system.

## 1. Momentum produces the spacetime curvature without a new state

From

\[
\mathcal L_u\alpha
=\iota_u\beta+d(\alpha(u))
\]

and, because \(\delta\alpha=0\),

\[
\Delta_H\alpha=\delta\beta,
\]

the momentum equation becomes

\[
\partial_t\alpha
+\iota_u\beta
+\nu\delta\beta
=d\bigl(B-\alpha(u)\bigr).
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal J_{\rm NS}
=-\partial_t\alpha+d\varphi,
\qquad
\varphi=B-\alpha(u).
}
\]

Now

\[
\begin{aligned}
d_{t,x}(\alpha+\varphi dt)
&=d_x\alpha+dt\wedge\partial_t\alpha+d_x\varphi\wedge dt\\
&=\beta+dt\wedge(\partial_t\alpha-d\varphi)\\
&=\beta-dt\wedge\mathcal J_{\rm NS}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak F=d_{t,x}\mathfrak A
=\beta-dt\wedge\mathcal J_{\rm NS}.
}
\]

Pressure/Bernoulli changes the spacetime potential through \(\varphi\), but the
curvature itself is exactly the pressure-free vorticity/current pair.

**Classification: Exact one-form Navier--Stokes identity.**

## 3. Material contraction leaves only the Hodge-adjoint current

Let

\[
V=\partial_t+u
\]

be the actual spacetime material vector.  Contracting

\[
\mathfrak F=\beta-dt\wedge\mathcal J_{\rm NS}
\]

gives

\[
\iota_V\mathfrak F
=\iota_u\beta-\mathcal J_{\rm NS}
+dt\,\mathcal J_{\rm NS}(u).

Because

\[
\mathcal J_{\rm NS}
=\iota_u\beta+\nu\delta\beta
\]

and

\[
(\iota_u\beta)(u)=0,
\]

\[
\boxed{
\iota_V\mathfrak F
=-\nu\Bigl(\delta\beta-\delta\beta(u)\,dt\Bigr).

\[
\boxed{
\left.\iota_V\mathfrak F\right|_{T\mathbb T^3}

}
\]

The one-form in parentheses annihilates \(V\), as it must because
\(\mathfrak F(V,V)=0\).

For Euler, \(\nu=0\), this collapses to

\[
\boxed{\iota_V\mathfrak F=0.}
\]

Thus the material direction is a literal characteristic direction of the Euler
spacetime vorticity curvature.  Navier--Stokes breaks that kernel condition by exactly
one already-existing object: \(\nu\delta\beta\).

**Classification: Exact material-contraction identity.**

Since the spatial Lie bracket is

\[
[u,\omega]_{\rm Lie}

the whole vorticity PDE is equivalently

\[
\boxed{
[V,\omega]_{\rm Lie}
=-\nu A\omega,
\qquad
V=\partial_t+u.

This has a direct de Rham meaning.  The one-form \(\delta\beta\) is the viscous
current, while

the \(A\omega\) appearing above.  Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{material curvature-kernel defect}=\nu\delta\beta,

\text{material--vortex commutator defect}=\nu d\delta\beta.

They are not two mechanisms.  The second is the exterior derivative of the first.

For Euler,

\[
\boxed{[V,\omega]=0.}
\]

So material-time flow and vortex-line flow commute exactly.

**Classification: Exact vorticity/Lie-bracket identity and exact de Rham

\Phi_t^*(\delta_{g_0}\beta)

\boxed{

Equivalently, in the PDE-sign convention used in the earlier material note,

Thus the previous material Hodge-heat theorem is simply the Bianchi identity for the

## 8. Viscous Kelvin circulation is Stokes on the same spacetime curvature

Let \(\gamma_t\) be an actual material closed loop and let \(\mathcal W_{[t_0,t_1]}\)
be the spacetime worldsheet traced by it.  Its tangent plane is generated by the
material vector \(V\) and the loop tangent \(T\).

By the material contraction law,

\[
\mathfrak F(V,T)
=-\nu\delta\beta(T).
\]

Stokes on the spacetime potential gives

\[
\int_{\mathcal W_{[t_0,t_1]}}\mathfrak F
=\int_{\partial\mathcal W_{[t_0,t_1]}}\mathfrak A
=\oint_{\gamma_{t_1}}\alpha-
\oint_{\gamma_{t_0}}\alpha.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\oint_{\gamma_{t_1}}\alpha-
\oint_{\gamma_{t_0}}\alpha
=-\nu\int_{t_0}^{t_1}
\oint_{\gamma_t}\delta\beta\,dt.
}
\]

Differentially,

\[
\boxed{
\frac d{dt}\oint_{\gamma_t}\alpha
=-\nu\oint_{\gamma_t}\delta\beta.
}
\]

Thus viscous Kelvin circulation is not a separate stochastic/topological mechanism
at the deterministic smooth level.  It is the worldsheet Stokes face of the same
spacetime curvature.

**Classification: Exact material-worldsheet Stokes consequence.**

## 9. The spacetime Pfaffian is purely viscous

In four spacetime dimensions,

\[
\mathfrak F\wedge\mathfrak F
=(\beta-dt\wedge\mathcal J_{\rm NS})
\wedge
(\beta-dt\wedge\mathcal J_{\rm NS}).
\]

The purely spatial four-form \(\beta\wedge\beta\) vanishes, as does the square of the
time-space term.  Hence

\[
\mathfrak F\wedge\mathfrak F
=-2dt\wedge\mathcal J_{\rm NS}\wedge\beta.
\]

But

\[
(\iota_u\beta)\wedge\beta=0,
\]

because it is the contraction of the identically zero spatial four-form
\(\beta\wedge\beta\).  Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak F\wedge\mathfrak F
=-2\nu\,dt\wedge\delta\beta\wedge\beta.
}
\]

The Euler current contributes **nothing** to the spacetime Pfaffian.  In the inviscid
case the curvature is rank two wherever nonzero and has the material/vortex
characteristic plane described above.

Since \(\mathfrak F=d\mathfrak A\) and \(d\mathfrak F=0\),

\[
\boxed{
d(\mathfrak A\wedge\mathfrak F)
=\mathfrak F\wedge\mathfrak F
=-2\nu\,dt\wedge\delta\beta\wedge\beta.
}
\]

The spatial part of \(\mathfrak A\wedge\mathfrak F\) is \(\alpha\wedge\beta\), so
the ordinary local/global viscous helicity law is the Chern--Simons transgression of

More explicitly,

\[
\boxed{
\partial_t(\alpha\wedge\beta)
-d\bigl(\alpha\wedge\mathcal J_{\rm NS}+\varphi\beta\bigr)

After integration over the torus this is

\frac d{dt}\int\alpha\wedge\beta

## 12. The new no-escape frontier

The theorem removes two tempting but false endpoints.

First, transport and diffusion should not be compared as independent mechanisms.  In
the actual material frame the Euler temporal curvature cancels identically; the
remaining defect is exactly \(\nu\delta\beta\), and its exterior derivative is the
material--vortex commutator defect.

Second, the raw size of that defect cannot diagnose escape: high-frequency Beltrami
heat modes make it arbitrarily large while remaining globally smooth and preserving
the material--vortex foliation up to a scalar rescaling of its vortex generator.

What remains is therefore more specific:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{Can the self-reconstructed velocity and vorticity repeatedly generate a}\\
\text{transverse, phase-coherent material--vortex noncommutation cascade in finite}\\
\text{time, while the same one-form }\delta\beta\text{ simultaneously supplies the}\\
\text{material curvature defect, its exterior derivative, Hodge heat, Kelvin leakage,}\\
\text{and the viscous Bochner/carré-du-champ square?}
\end{gathered}
}
\]

A true no-escape theorem must use this **simultaneous self-generation**.  It cannot
be a bound on one raw curvature magnitude, a shell score, or a separately modeled
cascade current.

No finiteness theorem for the required cumulative transverse coherence is proved
here.

**Classification: Open.**

# Energy-sphere Rayleigh/Hodge master law

## Purpose

The preceding Poisson--Casimir theorem reduces smooth incompressible Navier--Stokes
on the mean-zero divergence-free sector to

\[
\partial_t u
=
\Omega^\sharp(\nabla E,\nabla H)
-\nu\nabla\frac12\|\nabla H\|_2^2,
\]

with

\[
E=\frac12\|u\|_2^2,
\qquad
H=\frac12\langle u,Cu\rangle,
\qquad
C=\operatorname{curl}.
\]

That theorem also isolates the transverse Casimir defect

\[
B=(C-\alpha)u,
\qquad
\alpha=H/E.
\]

The present note asks whether even this two-functional/defect description is still
above the smallest state-generated operator law.

The answer is yes on every smooth nonzero state.  After separating the kinetic
energy radius from the unit-`L^2` direction, the complete Hodge-gradient ladder,
the Beltrami defect, the Euler tangent vector, the viscous shape drift, the earlier
tangent quotient, and the material Hodge commutator all descend from the differential
geometry of one scalar Rayleigh function

\[
\lambda(q)=\langle q,Cq\rangle
\]

on the unit energy sphere.

The shortest exact shape law obtained below is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\dot r
&=-\nu r\left(\lambda^2+\frac14\|\nabla_S\lambda\|_2^2\right),\\
\partial_t q
&=
\frac r2\,\mathcal K_q\nabla_S\lambda
-\frac\nu2\nabla_S
\left(\lambda^2+\frac14\|\nabla_S\lambda\|_2^2\right),
\end{aligned}}
\]

where

\[
r=\|u\|_2,
\qquad
q=u/r,
\qquad
\mathcal K_qv=P(q\times v).
\]

Thus the conservative and dissipative shape motions are not generated by two
independent complexity functions.  Euler is the state-generated skew rotation of
the gradient of `lambda`; viscosity is the sphere-gradient descent of the
Rayleigh square of that same `lambda`.

No regularity or no-escape theorem is claimed here.  The gain is a further exact
compression of the unresolved mechanism.

All differentiations below are on the smooth common core of the Hodge operators.
No claim is made that the unbounded Rayleigh functions define smooth maps on the
whole `L^2` sphere.

## 2. Universal Rayleigh first-jet factorization

Let `A` be any self-adjoint operator on a common smooth core and define its Rayleigh
function on the unit sphere by

\[
R_A(q):=\langle q,Aq\rangle.
\]

For tangent `v`,

\[
dR_A(q)[v]
=2\langle Aq,v\rangle.
\]

Projecting `Aq` onto the tangent space gives

\[
\boxed{
\nabla_S R_A
=2(A-R_A)q.
}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
Aq
=R_A(q)\,q
+\frac12\nabla_S R_A(q).
}
\]

This is a universal identity for a self-adjoint operator; it is not a Navier--Stokes
estimate.

Apply it to `A=C`.  Write

\[
\boxed{g:=\nabla_S\lambda.}

\[
\boxed{

\qquad
Cq=\lambda q+\frac12g.

\[

=rCq
=\lambda u+\frac r2g.

\[
\boxed{
B=(C-\lambda)u=\frac r2g.

There is also a direct ambient phase-space identity.  Since

\[
\alpha=H/E,

\[

=
\frac1E(Cu-\alpha u)
=
\frac BE.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{B=E\nabla_u\alpha,}

\[
\boxed{

Thus `B` is not a primitive state or score below the Rayleigh-sphere level.  It is

Its positive defect energy becomes

\boxed{

The signed-curl variance found in the preceding theorem is therefore exactly one

## 9. Spectral transfer and material Hodge motion are the same commutator defect

The operator `K_q` admits a de Rham/Lie-algebra factorization that is not visible in
the purely phase-space formula.

\[
\boxed{

=-\operatorname{ad}_qv,
\qquad
\operatorname{ad}_qv:=[q,v]_{\rm Lie}.
}
\]

On the mean-zero divergence-free sector, where `C` is invertible,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal K_q
=-C^{-1}\operatorname{ad}_q.
}
\]

Now let `F(C)` be any Hodge spectral multiplier for which the expressions are defined.
Since `F(C)` commutes with `C^{-1}`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
[F(C),\mathcal K_q]
&=
-F(C)C^{-1}\operatorname{ad}_q
+C^{-1}\operatorname{ad}_qF(C)\\
&=C^{-1}[\operatorname{ad}_q,F(C)].
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
[F(C),\mathcal K_q]
=C^{-1}[\operatorname{ad}_q,F(C)].
}
\]

Since `u=rq`,

\[
\boxed{
r[F(C),\mathcal K_q]
=C^{-1}[\operatorname{ad}_u,F(C)].
}
\]

This identity holds for the **whole Hodge functional calculus at once**.  It says that
the spectral commutator generated by the normalized Euler rotation and the

mechanisms.  They are the same operator defect after the canonical inverse-curl
identification.

For `F(C)=C`,

\[
\boxed{

=C^{-1}[\operatorname{ad}_u,C].

Consequently the nonlinear part of the scale-free enstrophy law becomes

\boxed{

Under the vector/one-form Hodge identification used in the preceding material

\[

Thus the earlier

No third independent scalar-jet mechanism has appeared.  The moving value, gradient,

## 13. The no-escape frontier after the Rayleigh-sphere compression

The latest Poisson--Casimir frontier asked whether Hamiltonian tangent motion can
drive Hodge complexity into finite-time escape while viscosity descends the squared
Casimir-gradient norm.

The present theorem makes the same question substantially more rigid.

The energy radius cannot increase:

\[
\dot r=-\nu r\mu\le0.
\]

Thus any singular Hodge escape must occur in the **unit direction** `q`, not by
unbounded `L^2` amplitude.

Euler itself cannot change `lambda`:

\[
(\dot\lambda)_{Euler}=0.
\]

Therefore an Euler cascade cannot move the signed curl barycenter directly.  At
fixed `lambda`, increasing the scale-free second Hodge moment is exactly increasing
the slope of the same landscape:

\[
\mu-\lambda^2
